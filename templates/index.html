Computer Vision Pipeline Inspector

Step-by-step image processing transformations from raw sensor capture to coin classification.

1. Original Capture
Input RGB Sensor: High-resolution frame captured from webcam or camera sensor under ambient lighting.
2. Grayscale & CLAHE
Contrast Normalization: cv2.cvtColor + Contrast Limited Adaptive Histogram Equalization (CLAHE) reduces shadow variance across coin faces.
3. Bilateral & Gaussian Smoothing
Noise Suppression: Edge-preserving Bilateral Filter removes high-frequency surface noise while keeping the outer circular coin rims sharp.
4. Canny Edge Detection
Gradient Vector Estimation: Dual-threshold hysteresis (cv2.Canny) isolates closed circular boundaries and suppresses spurious background gradients.
5. Distance Transform & Watershed
Cluster Separation: Euclidean distance transform (cv2.distanceTransform) calculates peaks of clustered coins to separate touching edges.
6. Detection & Classification
Multimodal Reasoning: Combines Hough gradient voting, minimum enclosing circles, HSV color tone inspection, and physical diameter ratios.
